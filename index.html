Choose your menu, and see the price as you go.

Raphael and Inês cook in villas and private homes across the Algarve. Pick a date, tell them who’s at the table, and choose a starter, main and dessert from their published dishes.

Each adult’s dishes

Choose a starter, main and dessert for every adult, and mark allergies or dietary needs. Dishes come from their published sample menus.

Kids’ plates

Anything else?
